// how we started

Built from a frustration.
Grown into a movement.

Creative Minds Resource started with a simple observation: kids across Nigeria, the UK, North America, and Canada were surrounded by technology — but almost none of them understood how to build it. They were consumers of a world being shaped without them.

We started with a single class in Lagos. A handful of curious kids, a laptop, and an instructor who believed that coding could be taught to anyone if the right approach was used. Within a term, those kids had built their first websites. Within a year, we had school clubs running across three cities.

By 2026, we operate across four countries, run eight distinct tech programs, and have more than 500 students who have gone through our curriculum. More importantly, those students leave knowing they can build the future — not just live in it.

2020

First class in Lagos

A single cohort of 12 students, one instructor, and a Python curriculum written from scratch.

2021

First school club partnership

Greenfield Academy in Lagos became our first school partner. The Robotics Club launched with 18 students.

2022

Expanded to the UK

Our first international cohort launched in London. Web Dev and AI courses added to the curriculum.

2023

US & Canada launch

Partnerships established in Atlanta and Toronto. Student count crossed 200 for the first time.

2025–26

500+ students, 8 programs, 20+ clubs

Full curriculum now live across all four countries, with eight tech programs and over twenty active school clubs.

// mission & values

What we stand for,
every single day.

Make tech education accessible, exciting, and genuinely impactful for every young mind — regardless of where they live, what they look like, or what their parents do.

🎯

Outcomes over certificates

We measure success by what students build, not by what they can recite. Every course ends with a project — something real, something theirs.

🌍

Global perspective, local context

Our curriculum reflects what the tech industry actually looks like in 2026 — diverse, distributed, and moving fast across every continent.

🤝

Community over competition

Our students learn alongside each other, build together, and lift each other up. Tech is better when it's collaborative.

💡

Curiosity is the curriculum

Every lesson starts with a question students actually have. Curiosity is what got them there, and it's what we protect at all costs.

🔒

Safety is non-negotiable

Every instructor is vetted. Every environment is safe. Parents and schools trust us with their children and we take that seriously.

🚀

Ambition is welcome here

We don't cap what kids can achieve. Some of our students have gone on to internships, competitions, and businesses before they finished school.
