// why it works

School is where the habit forms.
We show up there.

After-school programs that require travel have drop-off rates over 60%. On-campus clubs run by us see consistent attendance and stronger outcomes.

📍

On-campus, every time

We come to the school. Students don't need transport, parents don't need to rearrange schedules, and teachers don't need to organise a thing.

📋

Curriculum-aligned

Every club program is mapped to STEM and computing curricula across Nigeria, the UK, US, and Canada — making it easy for schools to justify and report on.

👩‍🏫

Vetted instructors only

All our club leads are background-checked, trained educators who specialise in working with children and teenagers. Safety is non-negotiable.

🏆

Competitions & showcases

Club members get to compete in national and international tech challenges — giving them real stakes, real confidence, and real credentials.

📊

Progress reports for schools

We provide termly progress reports to school coordinators so they always know what students are learning and achieving.

💰

Flexible pricing for schools

We work with school budgets — from fully subsidised partnerships to split-fee models. Every school can afford to offer this to their students.
