Frequently asked questions

Do you offer online classes? ▼
Yes — all courses are available online and in-person where available. Online classes run via video call with the same curriculum and instructor quality.
What age groups do you teach? ▼
We teach kids from age 6 all the way to 17. Scratch is designed for 6–11 year olds, while courses like AI and App Dev are tailored to teens aged 13–17.
How long does each course last? ▼
Most courses run across a school term — typically 10–12 weeks with weekly sessions. Intensive options are also available during holidays.
Do you operate in my country? ▼
We currently operate in Nigeria, the UK, the US, and Canada — both online and in-person. Message us on WhatsApp with your location and we'll confirm availability.
What does my child need to get started? ▼
A laptop or desktop computer and a stable internet connection. No prior coding experience is needed — we start from absolute zero for beginners.
How much does it cost? ▼
Pricing varies by country, course, and format (online vs in-person). Message us on WhatsApp and we'll share full pricing details for your specific location and course.
